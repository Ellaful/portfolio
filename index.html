About Me

Ella Hung

3rd Year in The University of Melbourne, UX Design

Hi! I’m Ella, a UX/UI Designer who can also do Graphic Design and Video Editing. 

I have always been interested in human behaviour and how it shapes the way we think, feel and interact with the world around us. When I found out about UX Design and how design can shape our experiences with technology, I became interested instantly. Whether it's the calm satisfaction of a clean, elegant interface, the immersion of a well designed game, or the frustration and pain of not being able to find something quickly.

I’m especially drawn to UX/UI in games and narrative-driven media that emphasises engaging storytelling and enhances immersion through its design.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably reading, crafting, video editing, studying Japanese, playing video games, or rambling about my latest obsession.
