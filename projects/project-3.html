NEIRO MELT

He shares the Rat Room with Yodaka. Their room name is "Drowned Rat, Dry it" (濡れ鼠、乾く, Nurenezumi, Kawaku). Nagi chose this room because its name reminded him of the alias he uses when he calls into radio shows.[1] Nagi regularly falls from the 4th and 5th stories of buildings.
